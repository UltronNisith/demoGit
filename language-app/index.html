    How translation works now: everyday phrases match the hand-checked Phrasebook first (instant, offline, with pronunciation). Any other word or sentence is translated live — on-device in Chrome where available, otherwise via the free MyMemory online service. If the online daily limit is reached or you’re offline, the app falls back to its built-in word-by-word helper and labels it clearly, so you always get something. Live translations show a Reading line in English letters underneath — automatic romaji for Japanese, an approximate transliteration for Bengali — and your original sentence stays visible as a “You said” caption, like before. That reading is machine-made, not hand-checked like the Phrasebook, so ▶ Listen is still the best guide to the true sound. Speaking scores use your device’s microphone and speech recognition; tone is estimated from volume and pitch movement, a guide, not a native speaker’s ear.
