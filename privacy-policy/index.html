Privacy Policy — Language App Beta

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · App: Language App Beta (Android package com.teddy.languagebeta) and its web version · Developer: Saha (GitHub: UltronNisith)

Language App Beta is a free educational app for learning Japanese and Bengali from English or Hindi. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, or store your personal data on any server.

Text you translate

When you use online translation, the words or sentences you type are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a translation service — MyMemory (mymemory.translated.net) — only so the translation can be returned to you. The developer never sees or stores this text. On devices and browsers that support on-device translation (Chrome's built-in Translator), translation happens on your device and the text is not sent anywhere. The phrasebook, chat practice, and quiz work fully offline and send nothing.

Your voice and the microphone

The app asks for microphone permission only for the "Speak & Score" speaking practice, and only when you tap to speak. Your speech is converted to text by your device's or browser's speech-recognition service (for example, Google's speech service on Android/Chrome), under that provider's own privacy policy. The app does not record, save, or upload your audio itself.

Data stored on your device

Practice progress (such as quiz scores) may be kept in the app's local storage on your device so the app can show your progress. This never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a general educational tool and is not directed at children under 13. As described above, it collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
